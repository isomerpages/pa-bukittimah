<h1>Discover Bukit Timah!</h1>
<p>Welcome to Bukit Timah Community Club, a vibrant hub for residents to
connect, engage, and grow. We are dedicated to fostering a sense of community
through a diverse range of programs and activities, from cultural events
and workshops to fitness classes and social gatherings. Our goal is to
create an inclusive space where everyone can participate, learn, and celebrate
the unique spirit of Bukit Timah. Join us in building a stronger, more
connected community!</p>
